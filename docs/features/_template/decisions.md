# Decisions

Keep current decisions and concise rationale here. At consolidation, move detailed discussions and superseded decision bodies to history. Preserve each retired ID with its status, replacement link, and history link. Never infer approval from cleanup.

Record choices with lasting consequences, meaningful trade-offs, or enough ambiguity that a future contributor might otherwise reverse them. Do not record routine implementation details.

Allowed states: `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Rejected`, or `Superseded`.
